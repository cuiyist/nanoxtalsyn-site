Injection and precursor supply

Rapid injection can concentrate precursor conversion near the start of a reaction, helping distinguish nucleation from subsequent growth. A short period of rapid precursor conversion can favour nucleation, while a later lower supply can favour growth on existing particles.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Mechanisms of Nucleation and Growth of Nanoparticles in Solution — DOI 10.1021/cr400544s

Amine coordination

An amine nitrogen can donate electron density to suitable solution species or surface sites. Donation from the nitrogen lone pair and competition with other ligands can change precursor speciation and surface association.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Prospects of Colloidal Nanocrystals for Electronic and Optoelectronic Applications — DOI 10.1021/cr900137k

Phosphines and precursor solutions

Elemental selenium can react with organophosphines to form soluble phosphorus-selenium-containing precursor species. Organic substituents can make these species compatible with an organic medium, while their composition and reactivity depend on the phosphine and preparation conditions.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Recent Advances in the Liquid-Phase Syntheses of Inorganic Nanoparticles — DOI 10.1021/cr030027b
