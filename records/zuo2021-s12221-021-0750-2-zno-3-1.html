A hydrated acetate can supply soluble zinc

Zinc acetate dihydrate can supply a zinc precursor in a liquid oxide synthesis. Hydrolysis and condensation of the dissolved species participate in forming the zinc–oxygen network rather than simply copying the structure of the starting salt.
