Selenium delivery from TOP–Se

Trioctylphosphine selenide carries selenium in a soluble feed, rather than as separate solid selenium. The conversion of that feed can affect how much selenium is available while nuclei form and grow.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Synthesis and characterization of nearly monodisperse CdE (E = sulfur, selenium, tellurium) semiconductor nanocrystallites — DOI 10.1021/ja00072a025

A zinc carboxylate carries zinc into a nonpolar phase

Zinc stearate provides a zinc species compatible with a hydrophobic growth medium. Conversion of that feed and its binding environment can influence whether zinc reaches existing surfaces or contributes to other growth.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Prospects of Colloidal Nanocrystals for Electronic and Optoelectronic Applications — DOI 10.1021/cr900137k

A long-chain primary amine coordinates metal sites

During amine-assisted colloidal growth, octadecylamine can coordinate surface metal sites while keeping the surface compatible with a nonpolar liquid. Changing the capping layer changes which sites remain available for growth.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Oleylamine in Nanoparticle Synthesis — DOI 10.1021/cm4000476

Stable cluster intermediates can delay particle growth

In some indium phosphide routes, unusually stable molecular clusters accumulate instead of turning immediately into a smoothly growing distribution. Their appearance breaks the simple link between mixing the precursor and forming the final nanocrystals.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Two-Step Nucleation and Growth of InP Quantum Dots via Magic-Sized Cluster Intermediates — DOI 10.1021/acs.chemmater.5b00286
