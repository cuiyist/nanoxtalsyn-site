Microwave coupling and heating

Microwave fields couple to materials through their electrical response and can heat different parts of a reaction mixture differently. Polarization and mobile charges dissipate field energy, so heating depends on the liquid, dissolved species and other absorbing materials.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Microwave chemistry for inorganic nanomaterials synthesis — DOI 10.1039/B9NR00377K

Solvent and transport

A solvent carries dissolved species and transports heat through a reaction or dispersion. Solubility, viscosity and molecular interactions affect how precursors and ligand-coated particles move and mix.
