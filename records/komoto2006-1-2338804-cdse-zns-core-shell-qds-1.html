05

Chemical intuitionReferenced context and interpretation

Mechanistic interpretations and related literature remain separate from the experimental training labels.

No separately reviewed chemical-intuition essay is attached to this record yet.

Tributylphosphine as a chalcogen carrier

Tributylphosphine can react with elemental chalcogen to form a soluble molecular phosphine chalcogenide feed. When that feed supplies growing nanocrystals, its conversion rate affects the availability of chalcogen for nucleation and growth.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Molecular Control of the Nanoscale: Effect of Phosphine–Chalcogenide Reactivity on CdS–CdSe Nanocrystal Composition and Morphology — DOI 10.1021/nn301182h

A reactive organocadmium feed

Dimethylcadmium can supply cadmium directly in a hot-injection route instead of requiring an oxide to dissolve and form a complex first. Its different activation chemistry can change how quickly the mixture becomes supersaturated and nucleates.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Synthesis and characterization of nearly monodisperse CdE (E = sulfur, selenium, tellurium) semiconductor nanocrystallites — DOI 10.1021/ja00072a025

Technical-grade TOPO is not always chemically equivalent to purified TOPO

Phosphorus-containing impurities in technical-grade TOPO can change cadmium precursor coordination and surface binding during colloidal growth. They can therefore change shape selection without a change in the nominal solvent name.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: The Trouble with TOPO; Identification of Adventitious Impurities Beneficial to the Growth of Cadmium Selenide Quantum Dots, Rods, and Wires — DOI 10.1021/nl801692g

Cadmium phosphonates and elongated growth

Phosphonic acids can coordinate cadmium and alter the relative growth rates of the surfaces of wurtzite cadmium selenide. When the growth supply sustains that difference, elongation can compete with more uniform growth.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Mechanisms of the Shape Evolution of CdSe Nanocrystals — DOI 10.1021/ja0027766
