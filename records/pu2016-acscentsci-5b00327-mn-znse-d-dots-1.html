Chemical intuitionReferenced context and interpretation

Mechanistic interpretations and related literature remain separate from the experimental training labels.

No separately reviewed chemical-intuition essay is attached to this record yet.

A zinc carboxylate carries zinc into a nonpolar phase

Zinc stearate provides a zinc species compatible with a hydrophobic growth medium. Conversion of that feed and its binding environment can influence whether zinc reaches existing surfaces or contributes to other growth.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Prospects of Colloidal Nanocrystals for Electronic and Optoelectronic Applications — DOI 10.1021/cr900137k

Sequencing shell precursors separates growth events

Sequential cation and anion supply can bias crystallization toward an existing particle rather than promoting independent nucleation from a premixed solution. The result depends on how growth species reach the existing interface.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Synthesis and Optical Properties of Type II CdTe/CdS Core/Shell Quantum Dots in Aqueous Solution via Successive Ion Layer Adsorption and Reaction — DOI 10.1021/jp711395f
