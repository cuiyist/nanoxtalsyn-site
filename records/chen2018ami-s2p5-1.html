A thiol can affect both precursor and surface

A water-compatible thiol can bind precursor metal in solution and the metal-rich sites on a growing chalcogenide surface. Because these processes share the same ligand, acidity can jointly affect the growth rate and the coating that remains on the nanocrystal.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Thiol-Capping of CdTe Nanocrystals: An Alternative to Organometallic Synthetic Routes — DOI 10.1021/jp025541k
