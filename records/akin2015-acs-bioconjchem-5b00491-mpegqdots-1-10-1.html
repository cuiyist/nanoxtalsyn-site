Chemical intuitionReferenced context and interpretation

Mechanistic interpretations and related literature remain separate from the experimental training labels.

No separately reviewed chemical-intuition essay is attached to this record yet.

Zinc halide changes the coordination environment

A zinc halide additive can participate in the coordination chemistry of aminophosphine-based indium phosphide growth without being the phosphorus source. That environment can change the growth and surface chemistry of the resulting nanocrystals.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Aminophosphines: A Double Role in the Synthesis of Colloidal Indium Phosphide Quantum Dots — DOI 10.1021/jacs.6b01254

Stable cluster intermediates can delay particle growth

In some indium phosphide routes, unusually stable molecular clusters accumulate instead of turning immediately into a smoothly growing distribution. Their appearance breaks the simple link between mixing the precursor and forming the final nanocrystals.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Two-Step Nucleation and Growth of InP Quantum Dots via Magic-Sized Cluster Intermediates — DOI 10.1021/acs.chemmater.5b00286
