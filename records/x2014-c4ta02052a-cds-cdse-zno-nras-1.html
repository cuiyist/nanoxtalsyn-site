Thiourea can release sulfide in the bath

Thiourea is a sulfur-bearing precursor that can generate reactive sulfur species as the solution chemistry proceeds. Sulfur delivery coupled to precursor conversion can change the balance between surface growth and unwanted precipitation in the bulk.
