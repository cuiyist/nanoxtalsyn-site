Thioglycerol can keep particles dispersed in water

In aqueous cadmium chalcogenide growth, thioglycerol can bind surface cadmium through sulfur while the hydroxyl groups remain accessible to water. That capping layer influences the stability of the dispersion and the surface sites available for growth.
