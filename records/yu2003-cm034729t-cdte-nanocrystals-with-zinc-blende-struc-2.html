Cadmium oxide needs conversion to a soluble feed

Cadmium oxide is a solid precursor that can be converted into a soluble cadmium complex by an acidic ligand. That conversion makes it possible to control the cadmium delivered during nucleation without using a volatile organocadmium feed.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Formation of High-Quality CdTe, CdSe, and CdS Nanocrystals Using CdO as Precursor — DOI 10.1021/ja003633m

Nucleation and size focusing

A rapid change in precursor availability can create a short nucleation period followed by growth on existing particles. Growth can narrow the size distribution while material is supplied, whereas later ripening can consume smaller particles and change that distribution again.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Mechanisms of Nucleation and Growth of Nanoparticles in Solution — DOI 10.1021/cr400544s
