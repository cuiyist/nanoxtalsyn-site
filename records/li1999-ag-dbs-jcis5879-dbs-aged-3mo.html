Aqueous thiol-capped growth

In aqueous cadmium chalcogenide synthesis, a thiol with a water-compatible group can stabilize growing particles in the liquid. Solution acidity changes thiol speciation, so the precursor equilibria and the resulting surface coverage can change together.
