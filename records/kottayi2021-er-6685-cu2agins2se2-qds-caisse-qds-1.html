Chemical intuitionReferenced context and interpretation

Mechanistic interpretations and related literature remain separate from the experimental training labels.

No separately reviewed chemical-intuition essay is attached to this record yet.

A thiol can both supply sulfur and bind the surface

In metal-sulfide routes that use an alkanethiol as the sulfur feed, metal–thiolate chemistry can supply sulfur to the growing lattice. Thiol or thiolate species can also bind at the particle surface, so sulfur delivery and surface capping may be linked.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Selective Nanocrystal Synthesis and Calculated Electronic Structure of All Four Phases of Copper–Antimony–Sulfide — DOI 10.1021/cm5005642

A primary amine can change surface growth

Oleylamine can coordinate soluble metal species and bind exposed surface sites during colloidal growth. It can therefore change how rapidly precursors react and how readily the resulting particles remain dispersed.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Oleylamine in Nanoparticle Synthesis — DOI 10.1021/cm4000476

Nucleation and size focusing

A rapid change in precursor availability can create a short nucleation period followed by growth on existing particles. Growth can narrow the size distribution while material is supplied, whereas later ripening can consume smaller particles and change that distribution again.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Mechanisms of Nucleation and Growth of Nanoparticles in Solution — DOI 10.1021/cr400544s
