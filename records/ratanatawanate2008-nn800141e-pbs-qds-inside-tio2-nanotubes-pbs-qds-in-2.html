Acid can change capping-ligand binding

When used for acidity adjustment in an aqueous thiol-capped synthesis, acid changes the equilibrium between thiol and thiolate. Ligand protonation can change dissolved metal complexation and the colloidal stability of ligand-coated crystals.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Thiol-Capping of CdTe Nanocrystals: An Alternative to Organometallic Synthetic Routes — DOI 10.1021/jp025541k

Dissolved sulfide supplies sulfur for precipitation

Sodium sulfide supplies dissolved sulfide species for low-solubility metal sulfides in aqueous synthesis. Changing acidity changes sulfide speciation and can shift when precipitation overtakes complexation.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Recent Advances in the Liquid-Phase Syntheses of Inorganic Nanoparticles — DOI 10.1021/cr030027b
