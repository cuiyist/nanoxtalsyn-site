A strong reductant starts gold nucleation

Borohydride can turn a soluble gold precursor into gold rapidly in a two-phase thiol route. Formation of new nuclei competes with growth on existing particles, while the surface ligand helps limit coalescence.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Synthesis of thiol-derivatised gold nanoparticles in a two-phase Liquid–Liquid system — DOI 10.1039/C39940000801
