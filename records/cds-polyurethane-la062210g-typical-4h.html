Hydrated sulfide as an aqueous feed

Hydrated sodium sulfide dissolves to supply sulfide species for precipitation of metal sulfide nanocrystals. The acidity and metal complexation determine how quickly those species become available for nucleation.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Recent Advances in the Liquid-Phase Syntheses of Inorganic Nanoparticles — DOI 10.1021/cr030027b

Aqueous thiol-capped growth

In aqueous cadmium chalcogenide synthesis, a thiol with a water-compatible group can stabilize growing particles in the liquid. Solution acidity changes thiol speciation, so the precursor equilibria and the resulting surface coverage can change together.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Thiol-Capping of CdTe Nanocrystals: An Alternative to Organometallic Synthetic Routes — DOI 10.1021/jp025541k
