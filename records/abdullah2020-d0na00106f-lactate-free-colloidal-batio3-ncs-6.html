Alkalinity changes dissolved growth species

Sodium hydroxide can change which soluble hydroxide complexes exist during hydrothermal growth. That shifts the balance between dissolution and precipitation and can change the phase that crystallizes.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Hydrothermal technology for nanotechnology — DOI 10.1016/j.pcrysgrow.2007.04.001
