Primary source and SI ↗source

Cations can be replaced within a particle

Cation exchange can replace one set of cations while substantially retaining the anion framework of an ionic nanocrystal. The extent and location of exchange depend on ion mobility and the relative stability of the phases, so partial exchange can give distinct material domains.

General background, not the authors' interpretationReference: Forging Colloidal Nanostructures via Cation Exchange Reactions — DOI 10.1021/acs.chemrev.5b00739
