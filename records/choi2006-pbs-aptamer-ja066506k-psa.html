Measure the source assay

Add proteins to QD solution in a quartz cuvette while stirring; measure fluorescence with a liquid-N2-cooled InGaAs OMA V detector, 15 cm focal-length SpectraPro 2150i monochromator and 632.8 nm HeNe excitation. Exact volumes, stirring speed and assay temperature are not reported. Inject 5.7 µM PSA into 430 nM TBA-QDs and measure photobleaching kinetics (Figure S6).
